## Handoff

### 2026-09-29 — from T06 (delivered as `d28e0aa`)

**C1. The autonomy strings are fixed.** `autonomyFor(act, pairs, leaseAutonomy)` in
`core/src/delegate/autonomy.ts` takes `LeaseAutonomy = "earned" | "all" | null`, where `null` means no
active lease and nothing is live. Use `earned` and `all` in `awsf.delegate-lease/v1` exactly,
and pass `null` for a revoked or expired lease.

**C2. Act names are the policy's.** `--acts` should accept the strings in `PROPOSED_ACT_NAMES`
(`core/src/delegate/policy.ts`): `raise`, `resume`, `cancel`, `replacement-review`, `rework`,
`continue`, `degrade-review`, `route-fallback`, `wait-until`. Q1's prose names two of them
differently ("replacement review", "continuation"), and the code's names win because every
record and pair already uses them. `land-shadow` and `wait-for-owner` are never delegable.

**C3. The policy's lease view.** `PolicyLease` in `policy.ts` is `{ acts, fallbackRoutes }`,
and degrade-review is offered only when `acts` includes `"degrade-review"`. Map the lease's
`degradeReview: true` into that list rather than giving the policy a second flag.

**C4. What the grant screen and status can show.** `readLivePairs(taskRoot)` in
`core/src/delegate/pairs.ts` recomputes the task's pairs from disk. `autonomyFor` returns
`basis` (`code-decided`, `earned`, `owner-all`, `not-earned`, `no-lease` or `never`), the window
of at most 8 pairs, `matched`, and the count per source. Nothing is stored, so the screen shows
exactly what the next drive would read.
