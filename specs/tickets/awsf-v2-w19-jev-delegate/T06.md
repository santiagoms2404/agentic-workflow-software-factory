## What this ticket is

An **implementation** ticket. The owner's real acts are paired with the Delegate's earlier proposals, and each judgment act earns autonomy from that record.

The shared conventions (what Jev is, execution, the write boundary, the owner gates, the
never-do list) are in [`./README.md`](./README.md) and Section A of the build prompts, and apply
unchanged.

prompt because the sync fence compares everything after the prompt heading with Section B byte

## Handoff

### 2026-09-29 — from T05 (delivered as `364f15d`)

**C1. Where proposals are.** Each proposal is one `delegate.proposal` record
(`awsf.delegate-proposal/v1`, `core/src/delegate/proposal.ts`) in `<taskRoot>/delegate.jsonl`,
read with `readTaskDelegateProposals(taskRoot)` (`core/src/persistence/task-delegate.ts`).
`taskRoot` is `dirname(attemptDir)`. A record carries `attempt`, `sessionId`, `statusRevision`,
`stopKind`, `proposal.{act, rationale, executable}`, `decision.{recordId, outcome}`, `leased`
and the full `facts`. There is at most one record per `(sessionId, statusRevision)`.

**C2. Pairing by position.** The proposal does not record a journal `source_seq`. Every
attempt-journal record's `event.next` is the status at that point, so the stop's position is
the first record whose `event.next.revision` equals the proposal's `statusRevision`. The "next
act" is the first owner or host act recorded after it. The journal spells those acts as: a
`ceiling-grant` evidence record (raise); `resume-activation` (resume); a `transition` into
CANCELLED (cancel); a transition on L19 (rework), L25 (replacement-review) or L20 (land, the
land-shadow match); and `review-degradation` (degrade-review). Continuation happens in a new
task (`continuesTask`), not in the same journal.

**C3. wait-for-owner is not an act.** The table returns wait-for-owner for the judgment
fallbacks, the rationales `jev-unavailable`, `not-progressing`, `low-confidence`, `chose-wait`,
`chose-other` and `act-not-allowed`, and also for `ceiling-unreachable`, `quota-plan-absent`,
`quota-plan-stop` and `blocked-needs-owner`. Decide whether those pairs count toward agreement.
Counting them as mismatches would stall every judgment act.

**C4. Which rows are code-decided.** `ceiling-short`, `ceiling-funded`, `quota-recovered` and
`quota-reset-ahead` are decided by numbers. `jev-picked` and `lease-names-degrade` are
judgment. `leased-fallback-route` needs a lease. `isExecutableAct` in `policy.ts` is false for
land-shadow and wait-for-owner, and `autonomy.ts` should rely on it rather than restate it.
