never-do list) are in [`./README.md`](./README.md) and Section A of the build prompts, and apply
unchanged.

## Handoff

### 2026-09-29 — from T02 (delivered as `07e43d8`, `3417e81`, `6d85dba`, `0371f2d`)

**C1. stop-judgment's result is one input, not the proposal.** `STOP_JUDGMENT.policy` in
`core/src/decision/question-sets/stop-judgment.ts` returns `{ kind: "act", act, confidence }`, or
`{ kind: "wait", reason }` with `reason` one of `not-progressing`, `chose-wait`, `chose-other`,
`act-not-allowed` or `low-confidence`. Its thresholds, `progressingMin: 0.7` and
`nextActConfidenceMin: 0.85`, are both inclusive and match this ticket's wait-for-owner bars.
Read them from `STOP_JUDGMENT_THRESHOLDS` instead of restating the numbers. Your policy's own
numbers decide first.

**C2. v1 asks `risk` but does not read it.** The record keeps the score. Using it in
stop-judgment's policy would change what v1 means, so it would be `stop-judgment` v2. Read it in
`core/src/delegate/policy.ts` instead if the Delegate needs it.

**C3. `allowedActs` is what the stop offers.** `next_act`'s options are the caller's
`allowedActs`, then `wait_for_owner` and `other`. A pick outside `allowedActs` comes back as
`act-not-allowed`, so pass only the acts this stop can take.

**C4. An unavailable call has no `policyResult`.** Every outcome except `answered` returns
`policyResult: null`. Map it to wait-for-owner with reason `jev-unavailable` as the plan says.
Link the proposal to its record by `recordId`, and never make it a BLOCKED reason (INV-2).
