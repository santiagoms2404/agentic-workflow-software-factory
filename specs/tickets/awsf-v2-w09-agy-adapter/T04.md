## What this ticket is

A **milestone-closing** task. It flips THIS plan's markers in `specs/awsf-v2-w09-agy-adapter.html` and this ticket's `state:`. It does **not** touch the spine.

> **Optional workstream.** Nothing in v2 waits on W09. If a milestone cannot produce its
> evidence, closing unbuilt with the gap recorded is a legitimate outcome — the spine says so.
> **The route ends this workstream disabled.** Enabling it is a separate owner act in a
> protected file, and no ticket here performs it.
