## What this ticket is

A **builder** ticket, managed by default as one `build-review` attempt. Writable repository: this one. Markers and this ticket's `state` move by `awsf close` after the candidate lands, never in the candidate.

The shared conventions (the write boundary, the owner gates, "Closure, defined", the never-do list) are in
[`./README.md`](./README.md) and Section A of the build prompts, and apply unchanged.

The `## Handoff` section below is empty at authoring. The owner appends dated, numbered entries
(C1, C2, …) there after delivery, from the findings earlier tickets return. It sits above the
prompt because the sync fence compares everything after the prompt heading with Section B byte
for byte.

## Handoff

**C1 (2026-10-09, from T08).** Create the plan-author attempt with `--plan <registered spine stem> --ticket Wnn`; T06 seals exactly that spine ticket's registered path and digest. After landing, the owner chooses the approval row in `awsf close`; prose never identifies a row. The other implementation rows stay open, and the ticket derives `wip`.
