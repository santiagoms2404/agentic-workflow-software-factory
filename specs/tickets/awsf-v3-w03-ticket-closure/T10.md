## What this ticket is

The milestone's **Testing Strategy** ticket. It proves the milestone's rows. Writable repository: this one. Markers and this ticket's `state` move by `awsf close` after the candidate lands, never in the candidate.

The shared conventions (the write boundary, the owner gates, "Closure, defined", the never-do list) are in
[`./README.md`](./README.md) and Section A of the build prompts, and apply unchanged.

The `## Handoff` section below is empty at authoring. The owner appends dated, numbered entries
(C1, C2, …) there after delivery, from the findings earlier tickets return. It sits above the
prompt because the sync fence compares everything after the prompt heading with Section B byte
for byte.

## Handoff

**C1 (2026-10-09, from T07 and T08).** Refusal messages the journeys can assert. Attempt mode: 'closure requires a human actor'; 'closure requires an interactive owner terminal'; 'closure cannot run under a lease'; 'closure requires a LANDED attempt'; 'closure requires a sealed ticket selection and recorded base and candidate'; 'closure candidate is not an ancestor of canonical HEAD'; 'closure requires a clean canonical checkout'; 'closure ask changed since base: <ticket.field list>'; 'closure cannot resolve ask: <detail>'; 'closure delta refused: <path>: <detail>'; 'closure requires the checkout's configured user.name and user.email'; 'canonical HEAD moved during closure confirmation'; 'attempt changed during closure confirmation'; 'recorded closure is not in canonical HEAD'; 'interrupted closure: no published prepared commit; no automatic replay'. Owner-commit mode and roll-up: 'closure evidence commit is not reachable from canonical HEAD'; 'spine has no section linking to <plan>'; 'spine links to <plan> in more than one section'; 'spine section linking to <plan> must hold exactly one workstream task'; 'spine section linking to <plan> has no unique workstream ticket'; 'closure project differs from registered plan'; 'closure project journal identity differs'; 'closure requires distinct registered tickets'; 'project closure journal changed during confirmation'; 'interrupted project closure: rerun the original commit, plan and ticket selection; no automatic replay'. A shift closure offers only tickets the host recorded as built in the landed candidate; the rest print as not closable.

**C2 (2026-10-09, from G03-C, 8c12ec1).** The arm's usage text is `usage: awsf close <task> [--attempt <n>] | awsf close --commit <sha> --plan <stem> [--ticket <id>[,<id>...]]`. On success it prints `Closed <plan> <ids> in <short sha>: <n> row(s) closed, <m> left open.`, then `Nothing was pushed.`; on decline it prints `Closure declined; no byte was written and nothing was recorded.` and exits 1. `core/test/unit/closure/close.test.ts` already drives `main()` for five refusals and both forms on a unit fixture; the journeys in task 10 are the end-to-end proof on the stub route and should not duplicate those unit cases.
