### 2026-10-06 — Returned W01 M4 (K1) findings

- **C4 — The freshness rule is built.** `evaluateFreshness` in `core/src/preflight/fields.ts` returns `{refusal, field, reason}`. `refusal` is one of `no-record`, `other-attempt`, `stale-base`, `stale-config`, `stale-request` or `field-failed`, and `field` is non-null only for `field-failed`. Stale bindings name no K1 field, so `preflight-refused` and K2's `requires` must carry the refusal kind as well as the field. `git-storage` and `duplicate` are re-measured and override the recorded result; the suite is never re-run.
- **C5 — Reuse T09's gatherers, do not re-derive them.** `preflightConfigDigest`, `measureGitStorage` and `priorAttemptStatuses` are exported from `core/src/cli/commands/preflight.ts`, and `requestTextDigest` from `core/src/contracts/driver-preflight.ts`. The duplicate facts are built by the non-exported `journalFacts` in `preflight.ts`; export or lift it for start's re-measurement. The base chain (replay, then seed integration base, then HEAD) is `pinnedBase` in `preflight.ts` and must stay equal to `start.ts`.
- **C6 — Confirmations live on the attempt's own journal.** Read `request-confirmation` evidence with `readAttemptEvidence(attemptDir)` and pass every record, oldest first, as `confirmations`. `confirm` requires an existing preflight record, so the order is new, preflight, confirm, start.
- **C7 — A follow-up lands first.** The three carried M4 findings (suite reuse of the attempt's own record, broad `--where` globs over protected globs, and preflight reading confirmations) are fixed by a follow-up task before T11 builds. Read its landed diff, not this ticket's prompt alone, for the suite and protected-path behaviour.

### 2026-10-06 — M4 follow-up and G01-C landed

- **C8 — What the follow-up changed.** Landed as `9d0111a` and `d3d7742`. `gatherSuite` reuses landed rows at the base, then an earlier passing preflight record of this task or its continuation chain. `journalFacts` in `core/src/cli/commands/preflight.ts` reads no other task's journal, so start's re-measurement must not widen that scan. Preflight now reads the attempt's own confirmations.
- **C9 — G01-C is in.** `awsf confirm` is a registered owner act: the `confirm` arm in `core/src/cli/main.ts`, `OWNER_ACT_COMMANDS` in `core/src/lifecycle/next-steps.ts`, and the guard verb in both harnesses. It is not in `NON_TRANSITION_ACTS`, so `awsf next` does not list it at DRAFT. Adding it there, with `who: owner`, belongs with this ticket's K1 requirements on L1, and the next-steps test's verb list must move with it.
