## What this ticket is

An **implementation** ticket. It refuses known open defects before spend, or records them as fixed. No protected path, no gate. Writable repository: this one. Markers and this ticket's `state` move in the owner's bookkeeping commit after the candidate lands, never in the candidate.

The shared conventions (the write boundary, the owner gates, the trap contract, the never-do list) are in
[`./README.md`](./README.md) and Section A of the build prompts, and apply unchanged.

The `## Handoff` section below is empty at authoring. The owner appends dated, numbered entries
(C1, C2, …) there after delivery, from the findings earlier tickets return. It sits above the
prompt because the sync fence compares everything after the prompt heading with Section B byte
for byte.

## Handoff

### 2026-10-07 — Returned by T01, persisted with G02-S

- **C1 — Scope after G02-S.** #21 and #24 are a missing-placement gap: both stopped on an ENOENT for the project's `placement.yaml`, 0 calls spent, and only `awsf run` reads placement today (`composeProductionDesignContext`). Refuse a missing or unreadable placement before start, with a replay and a trap.
- **C2 — #12 and #14.** They are after-spend: `reworkPrompt` carries no earlier gate output, and the abort is the credential check on the provider's reassembled output after the call (`core/test/traps/replay/rework-after-spend.test.ts`). The defect stays here: rework scrubs credential-shaped provider output as a normal run does, instead of aborting to BLOCKED. Record their no-trap entries as `after-spend` with that mitigation.
