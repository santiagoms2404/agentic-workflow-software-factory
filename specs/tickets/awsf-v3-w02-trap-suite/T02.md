## What this ticket is

An **implementation** ticket. It turns the owner-confirmed seed ledger into the trap catalogue, with the cut and the population rule. No protected path, no gate. Writable repository: this one. Markers and this ticket's `state` move in the owner's bookkeeping commit after the candidate lands, never in the candidate.

The shared conventions (the write boundary, the owner gates, the trap contract, the never-do list) are in
[`./README.md`](./README.md) and Section A of the build prompts, and apply unchanged.

The `## Handoff` section below is empty at authoring. The owner appends dated, numbered entries
(C1, C2, …) there after delivery, from the findings earlier tickets return. It sits above the
prompt because the sync fence compares everything after the prompt heading with Section B byte
for byte.
