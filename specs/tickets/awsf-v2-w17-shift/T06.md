### 2026-09-25 — from T04

**C1. A pinned test turns red by design when you amend the rule.** In
`core/test/unit/shift-compile.test.ts`, "more than one ticket meets reviewBuildPhaseId's
single-producer rule, which T06 amends" asserts the current refusal for a three-ticket shift
(`phaseIds` = `t01-build,t02-build,t03-build`). Replace it with the amended behaviour:
`compileWorkflowStructure(...).minimumCalls === 4` and `reviewBuildPhaseIds` equal to those three ids.
A one-ticket shift already compiles, with `reviewBuildPhaseId === "t01-build"`.

**C2. Every shift builder has owner `builder`, but routes are keyed by phase id.** `production-run.ts`
resolves routes with `routes.get(phase.id)`, so one route per shift holds only while nothing overrides
a single `t<NN>-build`. That is the case your "one distinct provider" refusal must catch; the compiler
does not rule it out.
