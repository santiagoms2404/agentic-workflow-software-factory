## What this ticket is

A **testing and milestone-closing** task. It flips THIS plan's markers, closes its milestone's `<h3>` header, and writes that milestone's Amendment. It does **not** touch the spine.

> **Optional workstream.** Nothing in v2 waits on W10. If a milestone cannot produce its
> evidence, closing unbuilt with the gap recorded is a legitimate outcome — the spine says so.
