## What this ticket is

The **closing** task of the whole plan. It closes milestone M5, and it is the **only** ticket that writes to `specs/awsf-v2-plan.html` — or that deliberately leaves the spine marker alone when the workstream closes unbuilt.

> **Optional workstream.** Nothing in v2 waits on W10. If a milestone cannot produce its
> evidence, closing unbuilt with the gap recorded is a legitimate outcome — the spine says so.
