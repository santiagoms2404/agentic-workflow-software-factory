Rules for builders
Kinds and lanes differ by shape and fill. No role gets a colour of its own. Today builder, reviewer and plan-author each carry one in the config.
Orange marks three things: what spends, what waits on you, and a live lease.
Every number is read from a record. A missing value shows as a placeholder, never a guess.
A run that recorded a verdict shows its verdict first. Its lifecycle state sits in the detail.
Step lines come from templates. A model writing them stays parked.
The waterfall tries four levels before it scrolls: to scale, finished tickets compact, finished milestones fold, then the overview becomes the scroller. Lanes keep today's icons. See W6.
