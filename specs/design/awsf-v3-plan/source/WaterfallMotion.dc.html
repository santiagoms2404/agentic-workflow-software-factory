Rules the motion keeps
Time runs left to right. Nothing reorders, and nothing is hidden: a compact ticket still shows its brief, build, tests and any failed round.
The running ticket is the focus. It keeps at least 40% of the track, and its scale shrinks as it runs, so its bar always reaches now.
When a ticket's tests end and the next brief arrives, the finished ticket compacts to 1 px per minute, at least 48 px, and slides left in 400 ms.
Queued work waits at the right as 40 px stubs and moves into the focus when it starts.
Pressing a finished ticket moves the focus there and shows Back to now. The run keeps drawing behind it.
A wait on you longer than a tenth of the run folds into one orange gap that states its length.
